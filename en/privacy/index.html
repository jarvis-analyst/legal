Privacy policy for the Jarvis app

Last updated: 1 November 2026

In short: Jarvis collects no data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. It sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What the app connects to

Jarvis connects only to your own PC, which you paired with the app using a pairing code, over an encrypted HTTPS connection. There is no server run by the developer.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • The pairing key, in the iOS Keychain, on this device only and readable only while the device is unlocked.
  • The most recently loaded views, as an offline cache. They are unreadable while the device is locked and are not part of iCloud or device backups.

Deleting the app removes both.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only to scan the pairing code on your PC. No images are stored or sent. The microphone is never used.

Sample mode

In sample mode the app uses no network and stores nothing.

Other providers

  • TestFlight (Apple): Apple may collect crash reports, usage data and feedback you choose to send, and share them with the developer. Apple's privacy policy applies.
  • Network between iPhone and PC: you set up the connection yourself (for example with Tailscale); that provider's terms apply.
  • The Jarvis software on your PC downloads public data (such as filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission and prices) from data providers. They see which companies are requested, but no data from the app.

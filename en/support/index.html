Jarvis: help

Jarvis is a personal, rule-based analysis tool for stocks. The app shows what the Jarvis software on your PC computes from public data. Jarvis is not investment advice, connects to no broker and never trades.

  • Pairing: start Jarvis on your PC and scan the code it shows under “Verbinden” in the app.
  • Without a PC: tap “Ohne PC ansehen”. Sample mode shows the whole app with sample data.
  • Offline: recently loaded views stay readable and show their as-of date.
  • Feedback: in TestFlight with a screenshot or “Send Beta Feedback”, or by email to [CONTACT EMAIL].

This is a closed beta; there is no public support.
